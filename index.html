What to send

Complete hand-off, faster drawings.

Have these ready and your project goes straight into design.

  • Floor plans

    Clean CAD files, or original PDFs from the architectural set. No scans or marked-up copies.

  • Scope markup

    A marked-up set showing the work to be included on the drawings.

  • Bill of materials

    Every piece of equipment being installed, with part numbers.

  • Door hardware list

    The lock type at each door: maglock, electric strike, electrified lever or crash bar. Required even if hardware is by others.

  • Head-end location

    Where the panel or controller will be mounted.
