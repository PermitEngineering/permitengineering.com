What we draw

When a commercial project needs sealed electrical drawings for permit, we prepare the set for:

  • Tenant buildouts and commercial spaces, new and renovated.
  • Service changes, new circuits and equipment connections.
  • Power for signs, gates and low voltage equipment, including the circuits that feed fire alarm, access control and CCTV head ends.

What's in a typical set

  • Power plan showing receptacles, equipment connections and circuiting.
  • Panel schedules for new and modified panels.
  • Load calculations showing the service or feeder can carry the new load.
  • Riser diagram and general notes for the authority having jurisdiction.

Codes we design to

Electrical drawings are prepared under NFPA 70 (National Electrical Code) as adopted in the Florida Building Code, plus any local requirements of the building department reviewing the project.

What to send us

  • Floor plans. Clean CAD files, or original PDFs from the architectural set. No scans or marked-up copies.
  • Scope markup. A marked-up set showing the work to be included on the drawings.
  • Bill of materials. Every piece of equipment being installed, with part numbers.
  • Existing panel information if you are adding to an existing service: what is there now and where it is located.

How it works

  1. Send the project. Floor plans, scope markups and equipment list.
  2. Get a quote. A fixed price based on the scope and device count, before any work starts.
  3. Design and calcs. Drawings, calculations and code analysis for your authority having jurisdiction (AHJ).
  4. Signed and sealed. Permit-ready PDFs, digitally signed and sealed by a Florida Professional Engineer.
  5. Plan review support. We respond to reviewer comments until the set is approved.
